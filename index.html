02 — Private label & OEM

Your brand, our factory

Most of what leaves our unit carries someone else’s label. Send a tech pack, a sketch, or a sample garment you want matched, and we will quote against it.

Labels, tags and packing

Woven neck labels, printed care labels, hang tags, polybags and barcode stickers, all to your artwork. We can pack to a retailer’s carton spec.

Custom colourways and fabric

Pantone matching on dyed fabric, with trims, zips and tape sourced to match. Fabric weight and hand-feel chosen per order rather than fixed.

Decoration in house

Screen print, puff and high-density print, heat transfer, embroidery, appliqué and rubber or silicone badges.

Sampling before you commit

A pre-production sample is made and photographed for your approval before any bulk fabric is cut. Sample cost is credited against a confirmed order.

03 — Ordering

How an order runs

Four stages from first message to goods on the water. Each one ends with something you approve in writing.

1

Enquiry

Tell us the style, quantity, sizes and target price. You get a written quote with fabric options, unit price and lead time, usually inside 24 hours.

2

Sample

We make one sample to your spec and send photos and measurements. Courier the physical sample if you want it in hand. Changes are free at this stage.

3

Production

On your approval and deposit, fabric is cut and the run is stitched, finished and checked piece by piece. You get progress photos mid-run.

4

Shipping

Packed to your carton spec and handed to your forwarder, or shipped on our account by air or sea with documents sent ahead.

07 — Questions

Before you ask

What is your minimum order?

Fifty pieces per style per colour. You can split that across sizes however you like. For a first order with a new buyer we will sometimes go lower on a single colourway so you can test the market before committing.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes, and we recommend it. A sample takes seven to ten days. You pay the sample cost and courier, and we credit the sample cost against your bulk order once it is confirmed.

Can you copy a garment I already sell?

Send us the garment or clear photos with measurements and we will match the construction, fabric weight and fit. We will not reproduce another brand’s logos, trademarks or protected prints.

How do payments work?

Usually thirty per cent on order confirmation and the balance against the shipping documents, by bank transfer. For repeat buyers we are flexible. Letters of credit are possible on larger orders.

Will my own labels and tags be used?

Yes. Send your artwork and we will have woven labels, care labels and hang tags made locally, then show you photos before they go into production.

What if the goods are not right?

Tell us with photos. If the fault is ours we remake the affected pieces or credit them against your next order. We would rather fix it than argue about it.

08 — About

Made where sportswear is made

Sialkot has been making sportswear for the world for over a century. Eagle Mart works in that same supply chain — close to the mills, the trim suppliers and the printers — which is why we can quote a custom colourway on Monday and have a sample on a courier by the end of the week.

We started by selling our own tracksuits direct, which is still the fastest way for us to test a fit before offering it to a buyer. That retail side lives on Instagram. The export side is what this page is for: production runs, under your label, shipped wherever you need them.

We are a small unit and we say so plainly. You talk to the person who runs the floor, not a sales desk, and we would rather turn down an order than miss a date on it.
